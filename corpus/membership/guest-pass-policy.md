# Titan Fitness — Guest Pass Policy

**Document ID:** MEM-004
**Last Updated:** February 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## Eligibility

Guest passes are available exclusively to **Premium and Family** members. Basic members are not eligible for guest passes but may purchase a single-visit day pass for $20 at the front desk.

## Allowance

- Premium members: 2 guest passes per calendar month
- Family plan members: 4 guest passes per calendar month, shared across all registered family members on the plan
- Unused guest passes do not roll over to the following month

## Guest Requirements

Guests must:
- Be at least 16 years old, or accompanied by a parent/guardian if under 16
- Sign a liability waiver at the front desk before entering the facility (see Injury & Liability Waiver Summary, HS-001)
- Present valid photo ID
- Be accompanied by the sponsoring member for the duration of their visit

## Guest Access Restrictions

Guests using a guest pass have access to the gym floor, cardio equipment, and pool/sauna (where available at that location), but are **not** permitted to:
- Book or attend group fitness classes
- Book personal training sessions
- Bring a guest of their own

## Frequency Limits

A single guest may not be brought in more than 3 times within a rolling 90-day period. This is to prevent guest passes from being used as a substitute for a full membership. Guests exceeding this limit will be asked to sign up for their own membership or purchase a day pass at full price.

## Class Guest Passes

Occasionally, Titan Fitness runs "Bring a Friend" promotions allowing guests to attend a single group class free of charge. These are announced separately and are not part of the standard guest pass allowance.
