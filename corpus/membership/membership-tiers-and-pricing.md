# Titan Fitness — Membership Tiers & Pricing

**Document ID:** MEM-001
**Last Updated:** March 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## Overview

Titan Fitness offers three membership tiers designed to fit different training needs and budgets. All memberships are billed monthly unless an annual plan is selected at signup.

## Membership Tiers

### Basic — $39/month
- Access to gym floor and cardio equipment at your home location only
- Access during standard hours (5:00 AM – 10:00 PM)
- Free fitness assessment at signup
- No access to group classes or pool/sauna facilities

### Premium — $69/month
- Access to gym floor, cardio equipment, and pool/sauna at your home location
- Unlimited group class bookings (see Class Booking Policy, CLS-001)
- Access to all Titan Fitness locations nationwide
- Two guest passes per month (see Guest Pass Policy, MEM-004)
- 24/7 facility access where available

### Family — $99/month
- All Premium benefits extended to up to 4 family members at the same address
- Each family member must be registered individually with the front desk
- Children under 14 require a parent or guardian present at all times (see Minors Policy, FAC-004)
- Four guest passes per month, shared across the family plan

## Annual Plans

Members who select annual billing receive a 15% discount off the total monthly rate, paid upfront. Annual plans are subject to the standard cancellation and refund policies described in documents MEM-002 and MEM-003.

## Corporate & Student Discounts

Titan Fitness offers a 10% discount on Basic and Premium tiers for verified students and employees of partner companies. Proof of eligibility (student ID or employer verification) must be presented at the front desk annually.

## Tier Changes

Members may upgrade their tier at any time, with the new rate applied immediately on a prorated basis. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle and cannot be applied retroactively.
