# Titan Fitness — Refund Policy

**Document ID:** MEM-003
**Last Updated:** January 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## General Policy

Titan Fitness does not offer refunds for monthly membership fees once a billing cycle has started, regardless of facility usage during that period. This applies to Basic, Premium, and Family tiers alike.

## Annual Plan Refunds

Members on annual plans may request a refund for unused months under the following circumstances only:

1. **Relocation:** The member moves more than 25 miles from the nearest Titan Fitness location. Proof of new address is required (utility bill, lease agreement, or updated driver's license).
2. **Medical Exception:** A licensed physician certifies that the member can no longer safely use the facility. A signed doctor's note is required.
3. **Facility Closure:** If a Titan Fitness location closes permanently and no comparable location exists within 15 miles, remaining months are refunded in full.

Approved refunds are calculated on a prorated basis for the remaining full months on the contract and processed within 10 business days to the original payment method.

## Signup Fee Refunds

The one-time signup fee ($49 for Basic/Premium, $79 for Family) is non-refundable once a membership has been activated, even if the member cancels within the first billing cycle.

## Personal Training Package Refunds

Prepaid personal training session packages (see Personal Training Booking Policy, CLS-002) may be refunded for unused sessions within 60 days of purchase, minus a 15% administrative fee. After 60 days, unused sessions are non-refundable but do not expire.

## Disputed Charges

Members who believe they were charged in error should contact the front desk or billing support within 30 days of the charge. Titan Fitness will investigate and issue a correction or refund within 15 business days if the dispute is validated.
