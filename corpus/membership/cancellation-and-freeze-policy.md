# Titan Fitness — Cancellation & Freeze Policy

**Document ID:** MEM-002
**Last Updated:** January 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## Cancelling Your Membership

Members on a **month-to-month plan** may cancel at any time by submitting a cancellation request through the member portal or in writing at their home location's front desk. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle — Titan Fitness does not provide partial-month refunds for cancellations submitted mid-cycle.

Members on an **annual plan** who cancel before their term ends are subject to an early termination fee equal to 50% of the remaining months on the contract. Exceptions to this fee are outlined in the Refund Policy (MEM-003), including relocation and medical exceptions.

A minimum of **30 days' written notice** is required for all cancellations. Verbal cancellation requests made at the front desk are not considered valid until confirmed in writing.

## Freezing Your Membership

Members may freeze their membership for a minimum of 1 month and a maximum of 3 consecutive months per calendar year. During a freeze:

- No monthly fees are charged
- A one-time $10 administrative freeze fee applies
- Facility access is suspended for the duration of the freeze
- Group class bookings and guest passes are not accrued during the freeze period

Freeze requests must be submitted at least 5 business days before the next billing date to take effect for that cycle. Freezes cannot be applied retroactively.

## Medical Freezes

Members with a documented medical condition preventing them from using the facility may request an extended freeze beyond the standard 3-month limit. These requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by the location manager in coordination with the billing department, and members should expect a decision within 5 business days of submitting the required documentation. Extended medical freezes may run concurrently with a hold on an existing personal training package, and members with an assigned trainer are encouraged to notify that trainer separately so upcoming sessions can be paused. A doctor's note is required, confirming both the medical condition and an estimated duration of incapacity, and the standard one-time $10 administrative freeze fee is waived for all approved medical freezes, regardless of how far the freeze extends beyond the standard 3-month limit.

## Reactivating After Cancellation

Former members may rejoin at any time. If rejoining within 90 days of cancellation, the original signup fee is waived. After 90 days, standard signup fees apply as if joining for the first time.
