# Titan Fitness — Equipment Usage & Etiquette Rules

**Document ID:** FAC-002
**Last Updated:** October 2024
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## Time Limits

During peak hours (defined as 6:00–9:00 AM and 5:00–8:00 PM on weekdays), a **30-minute time limit** applies to cardio equipment (treadmills, ellipticals, stationary bikes, rowing machines) when other members are waiting. Staff may ask members to yield equipment after the time limit if a queue has formed.

## Re-Racking and Cleanup

All members are required to:
- Re-rack weights and return equipment to its designated location after use
- Wipe down benches, machines, and mats with provided disinfectant after each use
- Report any damaged or malfunctioning equipment to staff immediately rather than continuing to use it

Failure to follow re-racking rules after repeated staff warnings may result in a formal conduct notice (see Code of Conduct, STF-003).

## Personal Equipment

Members may bring their own equipment (e.g., lifting straps, resistance bands, gloves) but may not bring personal weights, external speakers, or personal mats onto the gym floor without prior approval from staff.

## Working In / Sharing Equipment

Members are expected to allow others to "work in" on equipment during rest periods, especially during peak hours. Refusing reasonable requests to share equipment is considered a violation of gym etiquette and may be addressed by staff.

## Dropping Weights

Deliberately dropping weights (other than during Olympic lifts in the designated lifting platform area) is prohibited due to noise and equipment damage concerns. Repeated violations may result in a warning or suspension of gym floor privileges.

## Footwear and Attire

Closed-toe athletic shoes are required on the gym floor at all times. Shirts must be worn in all areas except the locker room and pool/sauna facilities.
