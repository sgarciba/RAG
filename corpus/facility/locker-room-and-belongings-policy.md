# Titan Fitness — Locker Room & Personal Belongings Policy

**Document ID:** FAC-001
**Last Updated:** November 2024
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## Locker Usage

Day-use lockers are available free of charge on a first-come, first-served basis. Members must bring their own lock or purchase one at the front desk for $8. Titan Fitness is not responsible for locks left overnight — any lock remaining on a day-use locker after closing will be cut and its contents held at the front desk for 14 days.

Members on Premium and Family plans may rent a **monthly assigned locker** for $10/month, subject to availability at their home location.

## Liability for Personal Belongings

Titan Fitness is **not liable** for lost, stolen, or damaged personal property anywhere on the premises, including in locker rooms, the gym floor, parking areas, or pool/sauna facilities. Members are strongly encouraged to use a lock at all times and avoid bringing valuables to the facility.

## Lost and Found

Items found unattended are turned in to the front desk and held for 30 days before being donated to a local charity. Titan Fitness does not ship lost items to members; they must be picked up in person.

## Locker Room Conduct

- Photography and video recording are strictly prohibited in all locker rooms
- Cell phone use is not permitted in shower or changing areas
- Members must shower before entering the pool or sauna
- Shaving is only permitted at designated grooming stations, not in showers

## Children in Locker Rooms

Children under 6 may accompany a parent of either assigned locker room area. Children ages 6–13 must use the locker room matching their own gender identity or a designated family changing room, available at select locations.
