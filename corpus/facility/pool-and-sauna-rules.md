# Titan Fitness — Pool & Sauna Rules

**Document ID:** FAC-003
**Last Updated:** December 2024
**Applies to:** Locations with pool/sauna facilities only (see location directory for availability)

## Access

Pool and sauna access is included for Premium and Family members at locations where these facilities exist. Basic members do not have pool/sauna access, even when visiting a location that offers it, unless upgraded to Premium.

## Pool Rules

- Shower before entering the pool
- Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult at all times in the pool area
- Diving is not permitted in the shallow end
- Maximum pool capacity is posted at the entrance and enforced by staff during busy periods
- Food and glass containers are not permitted poolside
- Lifeguards are on duty during posted hours only; swimming outside these hours is done at the member's own risk where the facility remains accessible

## Sauna Rules

- Maximum recommended session length is 15 minutes per visit
- Members with cardiovascular conditions, pregnant members, or those under 16 should consult a physician before sauna use
- Shower before and after using the sauna
- Phones and electronic devices are not permitted inside the sauna
- Pouring water or any liquid on the sauna heater is prohibited except in designated steam rooms with an attendant present

## Hygiene Requirements

Appropriate swimwear is required in the pool at all times; gym clothes and street clothes are not permitted in the water. Members are required to shower with soap before entering either the pool or sauna.

## Closures

Pool and sauna facilities may be temporarily closed for maintenance, cleaning, or chemical balancing without advance notice. Titan Fitness does not offer membership discounts or refunds for temporary facility closures under 5 consecutive days.
