# Titan Fitness — Minors & Age Restrictions Policy

**Document ID:** FAC-004
**Last Updated:** September 2024
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## Minimum Age for Membership

Individuals must be at least 16 years old to hold an individual Titan Fitness membership. Members aged 16–17 require a parent or legal guardian to co-sign the membership agreement and liability waiver.

## Children Under 14

Children under 14 are not permitted on the gym floor or in group fitness classes, even when accompanied by a parent. This restriction exists for safety reasons related to free weights and machine equipment.

Children under 14 accompanying a Family plan member may use:
- The pool (with direct adult supervision at all times)
- Designated family changing areas, at locations where available

## Ages 14–15

Members aged 14–15 registered under a Family plan may use the gym floor and cardio equipment only when directly supervised by a parent or guardian who is also a member, and only after completing a mandatory equipment safety orientation with staff.

## Youth Fitness Programs

Select locations offer supervised Youth Fitness Programs for ages 10–15, run by certified staff during designated hours. These are separate from general gym access and must be booked in advance through the front desk.

## Childcare (Kids Club)

Where available, Kids Club childcare service accepts children ages 6 months to 10 years while a parent uses the facility. Kids Club has a maximum stay of 2 hours per visit and requires a completed registration form and up-to-date immunization record on file.
