# Titan Fitness — Personal Training Booking Policy

**Document ID:** CLS-002
**Last Updated:** January 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations offering personal training

## Session Packages

Personal training is sold in prepaid packages, available to any membership tier:

| Package | Sessions | Price | Price per Session |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 4 sessions | $220 | $55 |
| Standard | 8 sessions | $400 | $50 |
| Committed | 16 sessions | $720 | $45 |

Sessions are 50 minutes each and must be used within 6 months of purchase, except where a medical freeze applies (see Cancellation & Freeze Policy, MEM-002).

## Booking a Session

Sessions can be booked directly with a specific trainer through the app or front desk, subject to that trainer's availability. Titan Fitness recommends booking at least 48 hours in advance for popular trainers.

## Cancellation Policy

- Cancellations made **24 hours or more** before the scheduled session: no charge, session is returned to the package
- Cancellations made **less than 24 hours** before the session: the session is deducted from the package as if it occurred
- **No-shows**: the session is deducted from the package, and a $20 no-show fee is additionally charged to the member's account

## Two Consecutive No-Shows

If a member no-shows two consecutive personal training sessions without prior notice, their remaining sessions with that trainer are placed on hold, and the member must speak with the fitness manager before resuming bookings. This is intended to protect trainer scheduling and is not a punitive measure against the member.

## Trainer Changes

Members may request a different trainer at any time without penalty. Remaining sessions in a package carry over fully to the new trainer at the same per-session rate.

## Session Extensions and No-Shows by Trainers

If a trainer cancels or fails to show for a scheduled session, the member is offered a free makeup session and the original session is not deducted from their package.
