# Titan Fitness — Group Class Booking & Cancellation Policy

**Document ID:** CLS-001
**Last Updated:** February 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations offering group classes

## Booking Classes

Premium and Family members can book group fitness classes (e.g., spin, yoga, HIIT, strength circuits) up to 7 days in advance through the mobile app or member portal. Basic members may book classes on a **pay-per-class** basis for $15 per class, subject to availability.

Bookings are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis. Popular classes (spin, HIIT) frequently fill up 3–5 days in advance.

## Waitlists

If a class is full, members may join a waitlist. Waitlisted members are automatically booked in if a spot opens up and are notified via the app at least 2 hours before class start time, when possible.

## Cancellation Window

Members may cancel a class booking up to **4 hours before** the class start time without penalty. Cancellations made within the 4-hour window are considered a late cancellation.

## Late Cancellation & No-Show Fees

- **Late cancellation** (within 4 hours of class start): $10 fee charged to the member's account
- **No-show** (failing to attend a booked class without cancelling): $15 fee charged to the member's account
- Basic members who no-show a paid class forfeit the class fee entirely and are not refunded

Members who accumulate 3 no-shows within a 30-day period may have their class booking privileges suspended for 2 weeks.

## Exceptions

Fees may be waived for documented emergencies (illness, family emergency) if reported to the front desk within 24 hours of the missed class. Repeated fee waiver requests may be denied at management's discretion.

## Class Capacity and Substitutions

Instructors may occasionally be substituted due to illness or scheduling conflicts. Titan Fitness does not offer refunds or credits for instructor substitutions, provided the class itself still takes place as scheduled.
