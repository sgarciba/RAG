# Titan Fitness — Illness & Health Policy

**Document ID:** HS-002
**Last Updated:** May 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations

## Staying Home When Sick

Members experiencing symptoms of a contagious illness (fever, persistent cough, vomiting, diarrhea, visible rash, or a confirmed contagious diagnosis) are asked not to visit any Titan Fitness location until symptom-free for at least 24 hours without the use of fever-reducing medication.

## Class and Session Cancellation Fee Waivers

Members who cancel a group class or personal training session due to illness are exempt from the standard late cancellation fee (see CLS-001 and CLS-002) provided they notify the front desk or their trainer before the missed session, or within 24 hours after.

## Facility Hygiene Measures

Titan Fitness maintains the following standing hygiene measures across all locations:
- Disinfectant wipes and sanitizer stations at all equipment zones
- Enhanced cleaning of high-touch surfaces (door handles, machine handles, benches) at least twice daily
- HVAC filtration systems serviced quarterly

## Outbreak Response

In the event of a declared public health outbreak affecting a specific location, Titan Fitness will follow guidance from local health authorities, which may include temporary capacity limits, mask requirements, or short-term closures. Any such measures will be communicated to members via the app and posted at the affected location.

## Returning After Illness

Members returning to the facility after a reported illness are not required to provide medical clearance unless the illness required an extended medical freeze (see Cancellation & Freeze Policy, MEM-002), in which case a doctor's note may be requested before reactivating full facility access.
