# Titan Fitness — Injury & Liability Waiver Summary

**Document ID:** HS-001
**Last Updated:** August 2024
**Applies to:** All members, guests, and program participants

## Purpose

This document summarizes the key terms of the liability waiver that all members and guests must sign before using any Titan Fitness facility. The full legal waiver is provided at signup and is available on request at any front desk.

## Assumption of Risk

By signing the waiver, members and guests acknowledge that physical exercise carries an inherent risk of injury, including but not limited to muscle strain, joint injury, cardiovascular events, and injury from equipment use. Titan Fitness requires all users to consult a physician before beginning a new exercise program if they have a pre-existing medical condition.

## Release of Liability

Members and guests release Titan Fitness, its staff, and its trainers from liability for injuries sustained on the premises, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct by Titan Fitness staff.

## Reporting Injuries

Any injury occurring on Titan Fitness premises must be reported to staff immediately, and an incident report must be completed before the member or guest leaves the facility. Failure to report an injury at the time it occurs may affect any later claim.

## Waiver Validity

Waivers signed by adults are valid for the duration of membership. Waivers for minors must be re-signed annually by a parent or legal guardian and are automatically voided if not renewed.

## Emergency Medical Treatment

By signing the waiver, members consent to Titan Fitness staff administering basic first aid or calling emergency medical services on their behalf in the event of an injury, when the member is unable to consent directly.
