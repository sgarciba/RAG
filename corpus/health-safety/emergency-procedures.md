# Titan Fitness — Emergency Procedures

**Document ID:** HS-003
**Last Updated:** July 2025
**Applies to:** All Titan Fitness locations and staff

## Medical Emergencies

In the event of a medical emergency (collapse, severe injury, chest pain, loss of consciousness):

1. The nearest staff member calls 911 (or local emergency services) immediately
2. A designated staff member retrieves the on-site AED, located at the front desk of every location
3. Staff certified in CPR/First Aid provide assistance until emergency services arrive
4. All other members are asked to clear the immediate area to give responders space
5. An incident report is completed within 24 hours of the event

Every Titan Fitness location has at least two staff members certified in CPR and AED use present during all open hours.

## Fire Emergencies

Upon hearing the fire alarm, staff direct members to the nearest marked exit and to the designated outdoor assembly point (marked on the facility map posted near entrances). Elevators must not be used during a fire alarm. Staff perform a headcount at the assembly point and do not permit re-entry until cleared by fire department personnel or facility management.

## Severe Weather

Locations in regions subject to severe weather warnings (tornado, hurricane) will direct members to designated interior shelter areas away from windows and glass. Staff monitor local weather alerts during active warnings and will not permit members to leave the shelter area until the warning is lifted.

## Facility Power Outage

In the event of a power outage, emergency lighting activates automatically. Staff direct members off cardio and strength equipment with electronic displays and toward natural light near entrances until power is restored or the facility is evacuated.

## Reporting Near-Misses

Staff are required to log any near-miss safety incident (e.g., equipment malfunction that could have caused injury) even if no injury occurred, to support ongoing safety audits.
