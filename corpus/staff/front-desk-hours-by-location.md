# Titan Fitness — Front Desk Operating Hours by Location

**Document ID:** STF-002
**Last Updated:** June 2025
**Applies to:** Internal staff reference; also posted publicly at each location

## Standard Hours (All Locations Unless Noted)

| Day | Facility Access | Front Desk Staffed |
|---|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM | 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM |
| Saturday | 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM | 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM |
| Sunday | 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM | 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM |

Facility access outside staffed front desk hours requires a Premium or Family membership with 24/7 keycard access enabled (see Membership Tiers & Pricing, MEM-001).

## Location-Specific Variations

### Downtown Location
- Open 24 hours, 7 days a week for keycard-access members
- Front desk staffed until midnight on weekdays due to high evening traffic

### Suburban West Location
- No 24/7 access; facility closes fully at 11:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 PM on weekends
- Pool closes 1 hour before facility closing time

### Suburban East Location
- Reduced Sunday hours: facility access 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM only
- Front desk closed on major holidays (New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day)

## Holiday Hours

All locations operate on reduced hours (typically 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM) on New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Eve, and are fully closed on Christmas Day. Members are notified of holiday hours changes via the app at least one week in advance.

## Staff Shift Coverage Requirement

Each location must maintain a minimum of one front desk staff member and one CPR/AED-certified staff member on-site at all times during staffed hours, per the Emergency Procedures document (HS-003).
