# Titan Fitness — Member Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** STF-003
**Last Updated:** March 2025
**Applies to:** All members and guests at all Titan Fitness locations

## Expected Behavior

All members and guests are expected to treat staff, trainers, and fellow members with respect and courtesy. Titan Fitness is committed to maintaining a welcoming environment free of harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind.

## Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviors may result in a verbal warning, written warning, temporary suspension, or termination of membership without refund, depending on severity:

1. Verbal or physical harassment of staff, trainers, or other members
2. Discriminatory language or behavior based on race, gender, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic
3. Repeated failure to follow equipment etiquette after staff warnings (see Equipment Usage & Etiquette, FAC-002)
4. Unauthorized photography or recording of other members without consent
5. Solicitation or unauthorized commercial activity on the premises (e.g., freelance personal training not booked through Titan Fitness, product sales)
6. Bringing prohibited items onto the premises (weapons, illegal substances, outside alcohol)
7. Tampering with or vandalizing equipment or facilities

## Warning Process

Most conduct violations follow a three-step process: a verbal warning, followed by a written warning if the behavior continues, followed by suspension or termination for further violations. Severe violations (harassment, violence, safety threats) may result in immediate suspension or termination without prior warnings.

## Appeals

Members who believe a conduct action was taken in error may submit a written appeal to the location manager within 14 days of the action. Appeals are reviewed within 10 business days, and the outcome is communicated in writing.

## Termination Without Refund

Membership terminated due to a Code of Conduct violation is not eligible for any refund of fees paid, including prepaid annual plans or personal training packages, consistent with the Refund Policy (MEM-003).
