# Titan Fitness — Trainer Certification Requirements

**Document ID:** STF-001
**Last Updated:** April 2025
**Applies to:** All personal training and group fitness staff

## Minimum Certification

All personal trainers employed by Titan Fitness must hold a current certification from one of the following nationally accredited bodies:
- NASM (National Academy of Sports Medicine)
- ACE (American Council on Exercise)
- ACSM (American College of Sports Medicine)
- NSCA (National Strength and Conditioning Association)

Group fitness instructors must hold a certification specific to their class format (e.g., a recognized spin, yoga, or HIIT instructor certification) in addition to a general group fitness certification where required by state law.

## CPR/AED Certification

Because Titan Fitness locations often have no on-duty medical staff beyond trained employees, CPR and AED readiness among the training staff is treated as a core safety requirement rather than an optional credential. Every personal trainer and group fitness instructor, regardless of tenure or specialty, falls under this requirement, and compliance is tracked centrally by the corporate safety team rather than left to individual location managers. All trainers and group instructors must maintain current CPR and AED certification at all times, and this certification must be renewed every 2 years without exception. Trainers whose CPR/AED certification lapses are not permitted to lead sessions until renewed and are transitioned to non-training duties in the interim.

## Continuing Education

Trainers must complete at least 12 hours of continuing education credits per year to maintain their certification and their eligibility to train at Titan Fitness. Titan Fitness reimburses up to $150/year in continuing education costs for trainers who submit proof of course completion.

## Specialty Certifications

Trainers offering specialized services (e.g., pre/postnatal training, senior fitness, youth training) must hold an additional specialty certification relevant to that population before offering those services to members.

## Background Checks

All trainers and instructors undergo a background check before hire and are re-screened every 3 years, consistent with Titan Fitness's commitment to member safety, particularly around youth programs (see Minors & Age Restrictions Policy, FAC-004).

## Certification Audits

Titan Fitness conducts an annual audit of all trainer and instructor certifications. Staff found to be practicing with a lapsed certification are suspended from client-facing duties until the certification is renewed.
